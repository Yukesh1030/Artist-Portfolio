The Artist

The Sound Behind The Story

I am a composer and music director dedicated to crafting emotional soundscapes that elevate narrative storytelling. Over the past decade, my work has spanned cinematic film scores, intense background scoring, and intimate album production.

Musical Journey

Early Years

Discovered the intersection of narrative and music, learning classical piano and early orchestration techniques.

Musical Training

Formal education in sound design, synthesis, and orchestral arrangement, establishing a foundation for cinematic scores.

First Composition

Began composing original pieces for short films, realizing the profound impact of audio on visual perception.

Film & Media

Entered the film industry as a lead composer, crafting themes and motifs for critically acclaimed indie films.

Current Work

Now working as a full-time Music Director and Producer, leading large-scale cinematic projects and original albums.

Philosophy

Music should never exist just to fill silence. It is an invisible character in every scene. My philosophy revolves around finding the core emotion of a narrative and building a sonic landscape that resonates deeply with the audience. Every layer, every note, every moment of silence is deliberate.

Influences & Inspiration

Hans Zimmer Trent Reznor Ludwig Göransson Max Richter

Studio Practice

Analog Warmth

Live Recording

Spatial Audio
