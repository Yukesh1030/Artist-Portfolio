Frequently Asked

Do you work remotely?

Yes, while based in LA, the studio is equipped for fully remote collaboration. We can handle live tracking sessions remotely using AudioMovers and communicate via video sync.

What is your typical turnaround time?

It depends heavily on the project scope. A feature film score generally takes 8-12 weeks, while a single track production might take 2-3 weeks. We discuss timelines extensively before starting.

Do you mix and master your own work?

I mix all of my compositions and productions in-house. Depending on the release requirements, I either master the project or collaborate with specialized mastering engineers.
