The Silent Sea

Film Score • 2026

Client / Production

Aura Films

Role

Music Director & Composer

Genre

Cinematic Orchestral

Key Skills

Orchestration, Sound Design

The Story & Approach

The Silent Sea is a psychological thriller set in an isolated research facility. The director wanted a score that felt cold, vast, and increasingly claustrophobic. Instead of traditional strings, I focused on processed brass, deep analog synthesizers, and metallic sound design to create an atmosphere of dread.

The main theme uses a recurring 3-note motif that represents the crushing weight of the ocean above, evolving throughout the film from a subtle drone into an overwhelming wall of sound.

Neon Nights

Album Production • 2025

Artist

Vellichor

Role

Producer & Arranger

Genre

Synthwave / Alternative

Key Skills

Production, Mixing

Production Details

For this 10-track album, the goal was to blend nostalgic 80s synth textures with modern, hard-hitting drum production. We spent weeks sampling vintage hardware synthesizers to create a custom sound library before writing a single chord.

The arrangement process involved stripping back complex ideas to their most infectious grooves, ensuring every track felt like a midnight drive through a neon-lit city.
